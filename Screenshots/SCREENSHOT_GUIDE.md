# SATURN AR — Device Screenshot Capture Guide

This guide details the procedure for capturing the **8 required runtime demonstration screenshots** directly from your Android mobile device running `SATURN_AR.apk`.

In compliance with academic and project engineering guidelines:
- Mockups, simulated graphics, or synthetic renders are strictly prohibited as proof of working AR functionality.
- Real device screenshots must be captured following the checklist below.

---

## Prerequisites: Installing SATURN_AR.apk on Android

1. Transfer `SATURN_AR.apk` (located in the repository root or `Submission/SATURN_AR.apk`) to your Android device via USB, Google Drive, or local file sharing.
2. Ensure **Google Play Services for AR** (ARCore) is installed or updated on your device.
3. Open your device file manager, tap `SATURN_AR.apk`, and select **Install** (enable "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted).
4. Launch **SATURN AR** from your app drawer.

---
